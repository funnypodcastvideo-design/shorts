Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy describes how Shorts (“the app”) handles data. The app is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer to publish videos to the owner's YouTube channel, Funny Podcast Moments.

Data accessed through Google

When the channel owner connects a Google account, the app requests these permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos to the connected YouTube channel;
  • youtube.readonly: to display the name and picture of the connected channel.

The app does not read, collect or store any other data from the Google account: no e-mails, contacts, viewing history or personal information.

Storage

The access tokens issued by Google are stored only on the owner's computer, in a local file. They are never sent to any server other than Google's own APIs, and never shared with or sold to third parties.

Use of YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the app (“Disconnect”) or from your Google account permissions page. Revoking access makes the stored tokens unusable.

Contact

For any question about this policy, or to request removal of a clip, contact funnypodcastvideo@proton.me.
