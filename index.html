Shorts

Shorts is a personal, non-commercial tool used to run the Funny Podcast Moments YouTube channel. It turns long videos into short vertical clips with subtitles, then publishes them to that channel.

What the app does

  • Selects funny or interesting moments from long videos.
  • Crops them to a vertical format and adds subtitles.
  • Uploads the resulting Shorts to the channel owner's own YouTube channel, with credit to the original creators.

Google account access

The app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to the channel of the person who connected it and to display the channel name. It runs on the owner's own computer and is not offered to the public.
